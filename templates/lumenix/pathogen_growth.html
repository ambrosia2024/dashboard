Each region is coloured by its growth rate μ(T): how fast {{ index.label }} could grow at the region's daily mean air temperature, relative to its fastest. The scale is fixed from 0 to 1, so frames can be compared with each other.

With a week, month or year step, a region's colour is the average of its daily values in that period. Regions are whole NUTS2 areas with one temperature per day, so a warm valley inside a mild region is averaged away.

Year steps: a single year mostly shows that year's weather — year-to-year swings are nearly as large as the change over 30 years. Averaging over 10 or 30 years shows the climate trend; the frame labelled 1991–2000 averages those ten years.

Dashed outlines mark regions without data for the frame.

Calculated here
  • The growth rate μ for every region and day, from that day's temperature with the Rosso et al. (1993) cardinal model.
  • Week, month and year values: averages of the daily rates.
Assumed
  • {{ index.label }} grows between {{ index.t_min }} and {{ index.t_max }} °C, fastest at {{ index.t_opt }} °C (EFSA thresholds).
  • The daily mean temperature drives growth, not the daily maximum.
  • No effect of humidity, and no minimum number of warm days in a row.
Not included
  • Measurements of {{ index.label }} or outbreak data; the map has not been checked against them.
  • Where produce is actually grown: every region is coloured.
  • Uncertainty. It is not available, which does not mean it is small.

    Method, data and limitations
    • Model: the cardinal temperature model with inflection (Rosso et al., 1993), μ(T) = μopt · (T − Tmin)²(T − Tmax) / ((Topt − Tmin)[(Topt − Tmin)(T − Topt) − (Topt − Tmax)(Topt + Tmin − 2T)]), shown relative to μopt, and 0 outside Tmin–Tmax.
    • Cardinal temperatures for {{ index.label }}: Tmin {{ index.t_min }} °C, Topt {{ index.t_opt }} °C, Tmax {{ index.t_max }} °C ({{ index.source }}).
    • Temperature: the daily mean air temperature per NUTS2 region behind the pathogen model data. It comes from a single climate projection covering 1971–2095, which the source does not name, so there are no climate scenarios (RCPs) to compare.
    • Regional, not gridded: each region has one temperature per day, so local hot spots are averaged away. Gridded results could be added later as a second layer.
    • Daily mean temperature only; the daily maximum would give higher rates. No minimum duration (such as 2–3 consecutive warm days) and no humidity are applied yet.
    • Week, month and year values are means of the daily rates. Periods with too little data are left empty: weeks under 5 days, months under 20, years under 330.
    • Month and year steps read a monthly table that is precomputed from the same daily temperatures and rebuilt after each data sync.

    References

    • Rosso et al., 1993: the cardinal temperature model.
    • Stathas et al., 2026: using the cardinal model in place of climate indices.
    • EFSA thresholds for {{ index.label }} in fresh produce: Tmin {{ index.t_min }} °C, Topt {{ index.t_opt }} °C, Tmax {{ index.t_max }} °C.
